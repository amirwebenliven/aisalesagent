The headline correction

It costs about twice the advertised rate

The published rule is "one AI response, one credit charge." The actual ledger tells a different story: every AI action is billed separately — not just the reply the customer sees. One real conversation on 15 August produced eight charges for four visible replies.

Read the 18.06 carefully. It's inflated by fixed overhead — knowledge-base ingestion and daily summaries — spread across only three or four real chats. The defensible planning figure from the conversational lines alone is roughly 0.5–0.6 credits per AI reply, about double the headline, plus ~1 credit per knowledge-base page at setup, plus ~7.5 credits/month passive per workspace.

Practically: budget about $27.50 per client per month at 500 replies, not the ~$12.50 the advertised rate implies. The account's own warning reads: "≈ 2 more chats on your balance."

The second correction

The cheap option isn't available to us

Bring-your-own-key only zeroes credits on the Pro tier. Max and Mini run on DM Champ's own infrastructure, so your key changes nothing there.

TierCredits per actionWith our own Anthropic key
Pro (Claude Sonnet)1.000 credits — we'd pay Anthropic ~$0.005/call directly
Max — what we're running0.25Still 0.25 — key is irrelevant
Mini0.15Still 0.15

So the counter-intuitive right answer is to run the most expensive-looking tier: Pro at 1 credit becomes 0 credits with our own key, at roughly five times cheaper than Max.

But we can't do it today

The agent editor's AI quality panel offers exactly three buttons — Pro ("Old reliable — 1 credit per action"), Max ("Recommended… 0.25 credits per action"), Mini — and no API-key field anywhere. Settings has no BYOK section either.

That option requires an Agency-tier plan. Until the account question below is settled, our real cost floor is Max at 0.25 credits per billed action.

The question that blocks everything

Are we the agency, or somebody's client?

The billing page shows:

  • Monthly AI credits: — (none)
  • Purchased credits: 100
  • Card on file: No
  • Subscription: Active

Plan features listed: unlimited messaging channels, unlimited contacts, contact tagging, 100K AI agent context, real-time web search, voice/image/video/document understanding, AI media library, tasks, webhooks, custom functions, advanced mode, reply to comments, automatic follow-ups, unlimited AI responses per chat, AI appointment booking, daily summaries, API access, 3 team seats.

Conspicuously absent — from both the feature list and the settings navigation: white-label or custom-domain settings, sub-accounts, any agency section, and AI model / BYOK controls. Settings contains only: Profile, Security & General, Business, Team, Billing, Tags, Channels, Short Links, Booking & Calendar, Webhooks, API Key, Notifications, Data Export.

The string "Sub Account" does appear in the settings page text.

Two readings, opposite conclusions

1. This workspace is a sub-account inside someone else's agency. Someone sold the boss a white-labeled instance. If so, we cannot resell from this account — we are the client, not the agency, and the reseller economics don't apply to us yet.

2. The boss holds an AppSumo lifetime deal — 3 team seats plus 100K context and no monthly credits fits an LTD tier — and configured white-label from a separate agency-level login, with this being his own working workspace.

This must be settled before any plan is built on it. It is now more important than anything about AI quality.

A one-click fix worth trying

Response speed is set to "Fast", not the default "Human Like". The interface warns in-line: "Faster response speeds reduce AI thinking time, which can lower reply quality. 'Human Like' is the most accurate." That's a plausible contributor to the 0% rate, and it costs nothing to change.

On a sample of three conversations, zero autonomous closes isn't damning — but it does not yet demonstrate the autonomous close the marketing sells.
