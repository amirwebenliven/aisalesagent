Branding

What white-label covers

When configured, zero DM Champ branding is visible to clients. Their own documentation puts it as: clients "have no idea they are using the platform under the hood." We confirmed this holds in practice — see part 4.

Visual identity

  • App name and company legal name
  • Logo with a separate dark-mode version, favicon, app icon
  • Primary, accent, success and danger colours
  • Heading and body fonts
  • Six pre-made themes (Nebula, Bloom, Ember, Tidal, Mono, Original)
  • Page backgrounds, card styles, corner radius, layout density

Everything else

  • Login page tagline and background
  • Outbound emails: logo, colour, footer tagline, up to four custom links, default language
  • SEO metadata, custom Terms/Privacy URLs, support email
  • Hideable: help menu, API reference, changelog
  • A domain can be narrowed to expose only certain modules

Requires the Agency tier or above, or AppSumo tiers 3–6. Not available on the Business plan.

Setup

Custom domain

  1. Enter the subdomain (app.youragency.com) in the Custom domain card
  2. Add a CNAME at the registrar pointing to tenants.youraiconnector.com
  3. The platform detects DNS and auto-provisions SSL via Let's Encrypt within minutes to hours

Separate branded domains are available for docs, API and MCP endpoints, each with its own certificate. Cloudflare gotcha: SSL/TLS must be set to "Full (strict)" or you get redirect errors.

Email sending can stay on their infrastructure with our branding, or route through our own Mailgun/SMTP (Postmark, SES, SendGrid, Resend). Different client groups can use different sender identities.

The CNAME target tenants.youraiconnector.com is an interesting tell — it suggests the multi-tenant layer may be shared with, or spun out of, a separate product. Noted, but not decision-relevant.

Client management

Sub-accounts

Creating a client is a three-step modal: account details (name + email, which becomes their login) → business info (name, address, country, language, timezone) → features (channels, AI capabilities, contact limits, seats, optional setup wizard). You choose whether to email the client their credentials or hold them yourself.

What the agency gets

  • Sub-accounts table with credit usage and campaign status
  • "Sign in as user" — support a client without knowing their password
  • Read-only inbox view per sub-account
  • Bulk-copy campaigns and agents into client accounts
  • Snapshots — package an entire setup as a reusable template and deploy to many clients at once
  • Credit allocation, notification routing, access controls
  • Soft/hard block a client without deleting their data

What the client sees

  • Their own contacts, campaigns and AI agents
  • A billing page — reselling mode only
  • Team management
  • Nothing we've hidden via Menu Visibility
The clever bit

How the markup works

A sub-account's "spending limit" is a cap on how much of our credit pool that client may spend — not a separate pot handed to them. Every client action deducts from both their limit and our agency balance at once.

On top of that, we set a per-action rate per sub-account. Their worked example: a client burns 0.5 credits per action while our pool pays 0.2. That 2.5× spread is our margin, applied automatically, with no reconciliation on our side and without pricing clients at a loss.

Recommended start

Managed mode is the right starting point for us — it works where Stripe is awkward, fits existing invoicing, keeps full control while we learn the product, and keeps the client payment relationship ours (which is also the platform-risk mitigation in part 6). Reselling mode is the scale play once the offer is proven.

Stated restrictions

  • A single sub-account cannot be shared across multiple brands
  • Client pricing must stay above platform minimums — the minimums aren't published
  • When a client's credits run out, the AI stops until they buy more
Why this matters

What we'd be rebuilding

If we build our own, this entire layer is on us, and it is not small:

  • Multi-tenant data isolation
  • Per-tenant theming — colours, fonts, logos, themes, login pages, email templates
  • Custom domain onboarding with automated ACME/Let's Encrypt issuance and renewal
  • "Sign in as user" impersonation with a proper audit trail
  • A credit ledger with per-tenant markup rates, spending caps and atomic double-deduction
  • Stripe Connect billing: plans, trials, invoices, auto-recharge webhooks
  • Snapshot export/import across tenants
  • Per-tenant notification routing and granular menu visibility

Realistically three to five months of work on its own, entirely separate from the AI agent and the messaging integrations. Unglamorous, security-sensitive plumbing — and exactly what we'd be renting for $248.50 a month.
